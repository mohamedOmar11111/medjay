# Growth areas coverage map (61 areas x 6 jobs)

Date: 2026-10-17. Built from a web-search pass (Reforge/Balfour, HubSpot, Andrew Chen, Think with Google, Lenny's Newsletter, CXL, Growth Design, Sean Ellis) plus the owner's real workspace patterns. **Only about 17 of the 61 areas have source support in the searches ([V]); the rest come from the researcher's general knowledge ([U], unverified).** HubSpot percentages came from summary pages, not the official report; verify before citing. Treat this as a scoping list, not evidence.

Automation key: **A** = agent-automatable (research, drafting, analysis, monitoring); **H** = needs human, creative work, or spend; **A/H** = mixed.

## The six jobs
Every area is served by some subset of six jobs. Build the jobs once; apply them per area via skill packs.

| Job | Meaning | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| audit | Find what is wrong or wasted, with evidence | Findings with source and date |
| research | Gather external or internal intelligence | Brief with cited sources |
| draft | Produce a deliverable for human review | Copy, plan, creative brief, patch |
| monitor | Watch a metric or source continuously | Alerts, pulses |
| analyze | Explain performance, attribution, causes | Diagnosis, ranked hypotheses |
| experiment | Test a tactic with a hypothesis and a metric | Tested-Tactics Log entry |

## Product-scoping takeaways
- Strongest agent fit: research, audits, drafting, monitoring, analysis.
- Humans must keep: spend approval, brand/creative decisions, sending outreach, measurement tests needing real holdouts.
- Business-type selection (B2B, B2C, ecommerce, app, services) turns the relevant areas on.
